How it works

A daemon is an agent with a job, a schedule, a memory and, when it needs one, its own computer. You talk to it. It does the rest.

talk

Ask

In the web chat or Telegram. It looks things up live and answers with what it found, not what it guesses.

summon

Hand off

When something needs watching or digging, it proposes a daemon. One tap starts it.

run

It moves in

It checks on its schedule, keeps notes between runs, and works on its own Linux computer when the job needs one.

report

You get the work

Pings, reports and files land in Telegram. Anything that spends money waits for your tap.

Tireless.
Not unsupervised.

  • read_only public data only. no logins, no posting, no trading for you.
  • approve every payment waits for your tap in telegram. nothing moves without it.
  • spend_cap its wallet has a daily cap enforced onchain. only you can raise it.
  • kill /kill stops it before its next run and cancels pending payments.
  • log every run, command and result is written to its log. /log reads it.

Models and money

It thinks with OpenAI models. When OpenAI is down or the day's model budget is spent, it falls back to open models on Cloudflare Workers AI. The daily budget is a share of the last 24 hours of creator fees, with a floor.

Live numbers from /api/meta. Every model call is metered and booked as it happens.
