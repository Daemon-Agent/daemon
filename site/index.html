$ man daemon

      
Four commands. Then it's on its own.

    

    

      

        summon
        
Give it a job

        
Pick a template or just describe it. Watch prices, chase replies, research a market, file the boring stuff.

      

      

        leash
        
Set the rules

        
Schedule, tools, a spending cap, and what needs your OK. It can't give itself more.

      

      

        run
        
It moves in

        
Its own cloud computer, browser, memory and wallet. Runs 24/7, laptop open or not.

      

      

        report
        
You get the results

        
Results and approval requests land in Telegram. Approve with one tap, or don't.
